My Inspiration: PARINEETA BORTHAKUR

So today the topic for my very first webpage is THE QUEEN OF MY HEART. Do you know who is she ???She is none other than PARINEETA BORTHAKUR

Parineeta Borthakur is a well-known Assamese singer, actress, and businesswoman. She was born on 21 February 1985 in Assam. Her mother’s name is Rina Borthakur, and her father’s name is Probin Borthakur. She has two sisters, Priyangi Borthakur and Plabita Borthakur. Plabita is also a well-known actress and singer.
Parineeta is married to Gautam Chakraborty, who is a sound engineer at Meet Bros Studio. They have a child named Nyor. She and her family currently live in Mumbai.
From an early age, Parineeta wanted to become an actress.On the other hand her father wanted her to be a singer.So she is a well known singer as well.Throughout her career, she has worked in both the Assamese and Hindi entertainment industries.
She has appeared in several Assamese movies, including Gaane Kii Aane,The Underworld, Poley Poley Urey Mon and others. She has also worked in Bollywood films such as Bhool Bhulaiyaa 3,Force,Kurbaan and Saas Bahu Aur Sensex.
However, she is perhaps best known to television audiences for her roles in Indian television serials. She has appeared in several popular shows, including Swaragini,Pyaar Ka Pehla Naam Radha Mohan,Vasudha,Durga: Ek Atoot Prem Kahani,Spy Bahu,Bepannah,Pritam Pyaare Aur Woh and many others.
During the early stages of her career, she also appeared as a guest actor in several television series and crime shows, including Monisha Morge: Case Files,Crime Patrol,Saavdhan India, CID,Aahat and others. Some of these appearances are quite rare and may even be unknown to many of her fans today.
Parineeta also has a music album titled Parineeta Borthakur. The album is a collection of some of her early Assamese songs, sung and performed by her herself. Her musical side is another beautiful part of her artistic journey.

Her Dedication Towards Work

Parineeta is extremely dedicated to her professional life. She has expressed gratitude to God for giving her opportunities to work continuously and for allowing her to remain active in her profession. She has also consistently shown immense respect and appreciation for her co-workers, producers, and everyone who has been a part of her journey.
And, of course, her fans hold a very special place in her heart. She has always expressed gratitude for the love and support they give her.

Her Spiritual Side

When it comes to her spiritual life, Parineeta is a devoted follower of Lord Shri Krishna. For her, the whole world is on one side and her beloved *Babu* (Krishna) is on the other.
I believe this deep spiritual connection is also reflected in the way she treats people. From what I have observed, she carries herself with remarkable humility and kindness. She seems to value everyone around her and treats her fans with genuine warmth.
In fact, I have rarely seen a celebrity who interacts with fans with such sincerity. Even a small fan page with very little popularity can sometimes receive such warm and touching responses from her when the love behind the fandom is genuine.
I have even seen her apologize to fans when she misses their messages or DMs on Instagram. Moments like these may seem small, but they reveal something very meaningful about a person.
That is what makes Parineeta ma'am exceptional to me. There is something genuinely magical about the way she connects with people.

More Than an Actress

Her talents and interests extend far beyond acting and singing. She is also a professional lipstick formulator and dermatologist. She has her own cosmetics brand, **NYOR**, which primarily focuses on lipsticks and lip balms.
Through her YouTube channel, NYOR Lifestyle, she has also shared skincare tips, makeup-related videos, and other lifestyle content.
You can explore that side of her journey as well—it shows that she is not limited to just one profession. She has built herself across several different creative and professional fields.That really feels so inspirational for present youth generation.

Her Love for Animals 🐾

Another beautiful thing about Parineeta is her love for animals. She has shown great concern for stray animals and has worked to help them.
This compassionate side of her personality makes her even more admirable to me. Whether it is through her work, her spirituality, her interactions with fans, or her care for animals, there seems to be a common quality running through everything she does—**kindness**.
And perhaps that is what makes her so special.
She is not simply an actress, singer, or businesswoman.
She is Parineeta Borthakur—a person whose talent may make people notice her, but whose humility and kindness make people stay. ❤️
